Instruments and Validation Studies

The outcome instruments used in the IVAM-ED trial were validated for Brazilian Portuguese. Key validation citations:

SRQ-20 (Self-Reporting Questionnaire) Gonçalves DM, Stein AT, Kapczinski F. Performance of the self-reporting questionnaire as a psychiatric screening questionnaire: a comparative study with structured clinical interview for DSM-IV-TR [article in Portuguese]. Cad Saude Publica. 2008;24(2):380–390.

SF-36 (36-Item Short Form Health Survey) Ciconelli RM, Ferraz MB, Santos W, Meinao I, Quaresma MR. Brazilian-Portuguese version of the SF-36: a reliable and valid quality of life outcome measure. Rev Bras Reumatol. 1999;39(3):143–150.

SCI-R (Self-Care Inventory Revised) Teló GH, Iorra FQ, Velho BS, Sparrenberger K, Schaan BD. Validation to Brazilian Portuguese of the Self-Care Inventory-revised for adults with type 2 diabetes. Arch Endocrinol Metab. 2020;64(2):190–194. doi:10.20945/2359-3997000000213

PSS (Perceived Stress Scale) Luft CDB, Sanches SdO, Mazo GZ, Andrade A. Brazilian version of the Perceived Stress Scale: translation and validation for the elderly [article in Portuguese]. Rev Saude Publica. 2007;41(4):606–615. doi:10.1590/S0034-89102007000400015

MMSE (Mini-Mental State Examination) Standard education-adjusted cutoffs used in Brazilian clinical practice: MMSE < 24 for participants with < 5 years of education; MMSE < 26 for participants with ≥ 5 years of education.

Citation Language for Student Reports

When reporting results from exercises in this teaching case, use the following language to correctly attribute the source and characterize the data:

Analysis was performed on a synthetic dataset constructed to approximate the sample characteristics reported in Matzenbacher et al. (2026). The synthetic data were generated for teaching purposes using the simulation code in R/simulate-ivam-ed.R and do not represent individual participant data from the IVAM-ED trial. Full simulation documentation is available in simulation-assumptions.md.

Cite the primary publication when discussing the clinical findings:

Matzenbacher LS, da Costa FL, de Barros LGB, et al. Interactive virtual assistant for health promotion among older adults with type 2 diabetes: the IVAM-ED randomized clinical trial. JAMA Network Open. 2026;9(1):e2553508. doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.53508
